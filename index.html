IPPT Rep Counter — Prototype

Camera-based push-up / sit-up counting. No wearable, no sensor hardware — just a laptop or phone camera.

Tap "Start Camera" to begin.
Push-ups, camera in front: face the camera with both shoulders in frame at the top and the bottom of a rep. Sit-ups: side-on, full body in frame.

Runs fully in this browser tab — video never leaves the device. Needs a desktop browser (Chrome or Edge recommended), camera permission, and an internet connection on first load to fetch the pose model (cached after that). Push-ups are counted from how far your shoulder rises and drops (scaled to your own shoulder width when the camera is in front, or torso length from the side, so distance and height don't matter), and sit-ups from torso angle. Counting is based on detecting the actual top/bottom of each rep (not a single-frame threshold check), and it self-calibrates from your own first rep rather than relying on one fixed universal number. Start in the top position and stay in the same spot — watch the diagnostics line switch from "calibrating…" to "calibrated." If it still won't count after that and the diagnostics line shows "Delegate: CPU" or under ~8 fps, that's a hardware/browser bottleneck, not the counting logic — try Chrome specifically, close other tabs/apps, or use better lighting.
